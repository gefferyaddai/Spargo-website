# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two audiences, one site:

- **Shoppers** (index.html): people who want the cheapest prices on groceries, produce, household items, hardware and gas near them. Joining a pre-launch waitlist.
- **Stores** (store.html): independent local shops (grocers, corner stores, pharmacies). Owner-operators, not marketers. They're busy, skeptical of ad platforms, and judge a pitch in a few seconds of scrolling. The job: get their products and deals in front of nearby shoppers and see whether it works.

## Product Purpose

Spargo finds the best local price for what's on your list. For stores, it's a way to be discovered by shoppers who are already searching for what they sell, with simple inventory, deals and analytics. Success for the store page: an indie owner finishes the scroll thinking "this is easy, affordable, and will bring people through my door," and fills in the form.

## Brand Personality

**Friendly, sharp, local.** Neighbourly and warm, but confident about the numbers, like a good corner-store owner who knows their margins. Plain language, short sentences, no jargon. Never salesy or corporate.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing templates: centered hero, three identical icon cards, hero-metric strips with gradient accents.
- Enterprise/corporate retail tech pages (dense, navy, stock handshake photos).
- Anything that makes a small shop owner feel the product is "not for them."

## Design Principles

1. **One site, one voice.** The store page must feel like the same Spargo as the consumer homepage: same tokens, type and nav.
2. **Show, don't tell.** Real product screens and concrete numbers beat adjectives.
3. **Respect the owner's time.** Each section makes one point; the path to the signup form is always one click away.
4. **Local over corporate.** Warmth and specificity (real store names, real places) over polish for its own sake.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: text contrast, visible focus, 44px touch targets, labelled form fields. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
