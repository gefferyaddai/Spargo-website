The grocery price comparison app built for Canadian shoppers.

Spargo compares prices at the stores near you, so you can see where your groceries, household items and gas cost less, and buy your whole list in the fewest, cheapest stops.

How it works

Four steps to a cheaper shop.

  1. Set your location

    Spargo finds the stores within reach of you and ranks results by distance as well as price.

  2. Search or browse deals

    Look up a specific item, or browse today's best deals near you.

  3. Build your list

    Add what you need. Spargo totals it at nearby stores and finds the cheapest single store or two-stop split.

  4. Shop with confidence

    Get directions and store hours, and scan price tags in-store to check whether a deal is really a deal.

Availability

Calgary first, then Canada.

Spargo is built in Calgary, and that's where it launches. Starting in one city lets us get store coverage and price accuracy right before we expand to other Canadian cities.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is Spargo free?

Joining the waitlist is free and needs no credit card. At launch, Spargo will be a monthly subscription with a free trial. Pricing will be shared with the waitlist before launch.

When does Spargo launch?

Spargo is in pre-launch now and opens in Calgary first. Waitlist members hear first, before anyone else.

Which stores does it compare?

We're building coverage of grocery chains, independent grocers, pharmacies, hardware stores and gas stations, starting in Calgary. The full list of supported stores will be published at launch.

How accurate are the prices?

Every price shows when it was last confirmed, and shoppers can report a price that looks wrong so we can review it.

What happens to my data?

We only ask for your email to join the waitlist. Our privacy policy explains what the app collects and why.
