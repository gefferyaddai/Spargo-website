03The app

One app.
Smarter shopping.

Search products, compare stores, and discover more efficient ways to shop.

  1. A

    Search any product

    Type what you need and see prices at nearby stores, cheapest first, with how fresh each price is.

  2. B

    Compare the store, not just the sticker

    Open a deal to see the price, the regular price, the store, its distance and how long the deal lasts.

  3. C

    Price your whole list

    Add items to your list and Spargo finds the single cheapest store, or the best split across two stops.

  4. D

    Know a deal when you see one

    Scan or enter a price tag and Price Score rates it out of 100 against prices at nearby stores.

?Good questions

Before you join.

Where Spargo is today, what it will cost, and how prices are kept honest.

Is Spargo available yet?

Not yet. Spargo is in pre-launch and opens in Calgary first, then other Canadian cities. Join the waitlist and we'll email you the day it's ready.

Is the waitlist free? Will the app cost money?

Joining the waitlist is free and needs no credit card. The app will be free to use too, and optional Premium features add extras, like unlimited voice control with your very own Spargo AI agent.

Which stores does Spargo compare?

We're building coverage of Calgary grocery chains, independent grocers, pharmacies, hardware stores, gas stations and electronics retailers. We'll publish the full list of supported stores at launch. How Calgary grocery price comparison will work.

How fresh are the prices?

Every price in Spargo shows when it was last confirmed, so you can see at a glance how current it is before you make the trip.

I run a store. Can I list my products?

Yes. Local stores can list products, prices and deals and see how shoppers find them. Learn about Spargo for stores.
